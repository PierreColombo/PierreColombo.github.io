Pierre Colombo, PhD

I’m a research scientist at Google DeepMind, where I train Gemini models. My corner of it is the law.

Before that I trained multimodal models at Cohere, and co-founded Equall, where we built LLMs and agents for M&A due diligence and put them in the hands of large law firms. Before that I was an Associate Professor (maître de conférences) at CentraleSupélec, where I started the NLP group. It was a tenured job. I left it to build things.

What I built

  • LawSaulLM — the first LLMs built for law. 7B to 141B.
  • RetrievalColPali, ModernVBERT, BidirLM — multimodal retrieval: text, images and documents, one model.
  • LLMsEuroLLM, EuroBERT, CroissantLLM — Europe’s open multilingual models, up to 22B.
  • EvaluationInfoLM — a metric for generated text. AAAI 2022 paper award.

8,000+ citations, h-index 36. The rest is on Scholar.
